Privacy Policy

YanMore Grind is a local-first coffee brewing journal, grinder recommendation tool, and optional coffee diary. The app does not require an account, does not use tracking, and does not declare tracking domains.

Data Stored On Device

YanMore Grind stores app data locally on your device to provide coffee grind recommendations, brew records, calibration history, bean and grinder profiles, an optional coffee diary and sleep feedback, app settings, and local backup/export features.

Local app data may include:

  • Coffee bean names and roasting details you enter.
  • Grinder and espresso machine profiles you save.
  • Brew records, optional espresso basket profile details, tasting notes, flavor tags, ratings, and calibration history.
  • Drinks you explicitly add to the diary: time and time zone, beverage and source, ingredient estimates or manually entered caffeine amounts, optional milk type and amount, store name, and notes.
  • Optional sleep feedback: attempted sleep, approximate sleep onset and wake time, quality, recovery, and notes. You can turn off the diary's sleep association and delete saved sleep feedback.
  • Photos you choose to attach to a brewing journal record.
  • App settings such as language and appearance.

Ingredient amounts are estimates unless you enter an external value. They are not laboratory measurements or medical diagnoses. Version 2.0 does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Location

The current app does not request location permission and does not collect precise location.

Humidity, when needed for recommendation context, is entered manually by the user. Core recommendation, logging, sharing, and export features do not depend on device location.

Sharing And Export

Sharing and export actions happen only when you choose them. A full backup may include brewing, diary, sleep, notes, and photo data. Review the destination before sharing a backup. The app does not automatically upload these records to a YanMore server.

YanMore Grind does not mutate the recommendation model merely because you share or export a record.

Tracking And Advertising

YanMore Grind does not track you across apps or websites and does not use third-party advertising identifiers.

Accounts And Servers

The current app does not require an account and does not depend on a YanMore server for core recommendations, diary, sleep feedback, sharing, or export.

App Store Purchases

Optional Pro purchases and subscriptions use Apple's App Store and StoreKit. The app reads product information and verified transaction or entitlement status, including expiry and revocation, to determine access. Product loading and purchase restoration may require a connection to Apple.

YanMore Grind does not operate a payment server or collect payment-card details. Its purchase and restoration code does not send coffee diary or sleep records to Apple. When Pro access ends, existing records remain on the device; restoring an eligible purchase restores access rather than recreating deleted records. Backups and purchase restoration are separate functions.

Future Changes

If a future version adds cloud sync, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, server import, or other network services, this privacy policy and the App Store privacy disclosures should be updated before release.
