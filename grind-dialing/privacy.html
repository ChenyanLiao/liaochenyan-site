Privacy Policy

YanMore Grind is designed as a local-first coffee grind recommendation and brew-recording app. The app does not require an account, does not use tracking, and does not declare tracking domains.

Data Stored On Device

YanMore Grind stores app data locally on your device so the app can provide coffee grind recommendations, brew records, calibration history, bean profiles, grinder profiles, app settings, language preference, onboarding state, and local backup/export features.

Local app data may include:

  • Coffee bean names and roasting details you enter.
  • Grinder and espresso machine profiles you save.
  • Brew records, optional espresso basket profile details, tasting notes, flavor tags, ratings, and calibration history.
  • App settings such as language and appearance.

Location

The v1 app does not request location permission and does not collect precise location.

Humidity, when needed for recommendation context, is entered manually by the user. Core recommendation, logging, sharing, and export features do not depend on device location.

Sharing And Export

Sharing and export actions happen only when you choose them. Exported files or shared records may contain the coffee, grinder, recipe, espresso basket profile, tasting, and calibration information you selected to share or export.

YanMore Grind does not mutate the recommendation model merely because you share or export a record.

Tracking And Advertising

YanMore Grind does not track you across apps or websites and does not use third-party advertising identifiers.

Accounts And Servers

The current app does not require an account and does not depend on a YanMore Grind server for core recommendation, logging, sharing, or export flows.

App Store Purchases

Optional Pro purchases and subscriptions use Apple App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles payment and billing. YanMore Grind reads StoreKit product information and verified transaction and entitlement status, including expiry or revocation, to determine which features are available. Product loading and restoring purchases may require a network connection to Apple.

YanMore Grind does not operate a payment server or collect payment-card details. Your Apple Account and payment information are handled by Apple under Apple's applicable policies. The app does not send your coffee records to Apple as part of its purchase or restoration code.

When Pro access ends, existing coffee data is retained. Free limits apply and advanced data may become read-only; restoring an eligible purchase restores access, not deleted or missing coffee records. Local backups and purchase restoration are separate functions.

Future Changes

If a future version adds cloud sync, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, server import, or other network services, this privacy policy and the App Store privacy disclosures should be updated before release.
