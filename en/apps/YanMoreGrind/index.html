Complete user guide

01 / A fresh bag of beans

Finding a setting for a new bag of beans

The dose is unchanged and the target is still 36 grams. With a new coffee, the shot suddenly finishes in seconds. Turn finer and it may barely drip. A familiar recipe does not tell you which setting this coffee needs.

YanMore Grind grew out of the repeated adjustments a new bag of beans can require. It brings equipment, beans and actual brew records together so you can compare attempts. You can also skip recommendations and just record recipes and tasting notes.

04 / Bayesian calibration

Adjust guidance with measured brews

The same number on two grinders does not mean the same thing. YanMore Grind uses a personalized Bayesian model, combining eligible measured brews with beans, equipment and recipe context to inform the next adjustment.

Guidance changes with valid records. Sparse or conflicting feedback may require more measured brews. There is no promised number of cups to calibrate; a suggested setting still needs to be assessed by tasting the result.

Variety, roast, rest time, dose, distribution, tamping, temperature, pressure and equipment condition also affect extraction. Validate recommendations in the next brew; they do not guarantee flavor or replace taste.

01

Aroma and mouthfeel, recorded separately

The 2.3.1 flavor wheel has 8 aroma families, 108 specific aroma descriptors and 10 separate mouthfeel options. In a brew’s flavor notes, choose the wheel, then a family and a descriptor; search and list views are also available. Select across families and set each intensity separately: clear lemon, for example, with slight creaminess. These are personal tasting notes, not chemical measurements or an official flavor certification.

02

Buy it again, start a new bag

In 2.3.1, start a repeat purchase from a finished bean record. Basic details carry over; confirm the new bag’s roast and opening dates before saving. This creates a new bean record within the existing library allowance. The old bag stays finished and its brews are neither moved nor rewritten. Canceling creates nothing. Brewing the same bag again does not need a repeat-purchase record: select that bag and add another brew.

03

One bag: a timeline and a Pro workbench

Open a bean’s journey to revisit its brews in time order. In 2.3.1, the ordinary timeline, old-brew details and manual recording remain Free within existing history limits. Grouping by method, workbench favorite filters, two-brew comparison and quick recipe reuse are Pro features. Reuse opens a new draft; measure this cup’s yield and time again. Canceling adds no brew. Expiry does not delete brews, favorites or recipes, but professional comparison and reuse require Pro again.

04

Free and Pro espresso calibration

Version 2.3.0 added editable Ristretto, Normale, Lungo, Allongé and Turbo starting recipes. In 2.3.1, Free espresso prediction and feedback calibration use the standard 1:2 ratio, such as 18 g in and 36 g out. Other styles, templates and custom-ratio predictions require Pro. This limits prediction, not the ratio of an actual brew record, and does not rewrite old recipes. Pro still has method limits; it cannot automatically predict every pressure or flow profile.

05

Coffee calendar and composition estimates

Saving a new brew can also add its composition estimate to the diary. Selecting an old recipe in the calculator does not count as drinking it. The Pro calculator gives rough caffeine and cafestol estimates with assumption ranges—not laboratory measurements or medical advice.

06

Milk-coffee support: milk inventory

Finish one or several small bottles, or record milliliter uses from a larger container. Stock removed and milk added to a cup stay separate, with undo for the current operation. Inventory, costs and reminders are Free; Pro edits label nutrition and lactose information. Reminders do not assess food safety.
