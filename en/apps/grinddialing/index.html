01 / A fresh bag of beans

Yesterday’s setting may not suit today’s coffee.

The dose is unchanged and the target is still 36 grams. With a new coffee, the shot suddenly finishes in seconds. Turn finer and it may barely drip. A familiar recipe does not tell you which setting this coffee needs.

YanMore Grind began with these mornings of repeated adjustments. It brings equipment, beans and actual brews together, so the next attempt has something to refer to. Or skip the recommendations and simply keep the recipes and tastes you want to remember.

02 / Watch the flow

Fast, slow—or close to the cup you had in mind?

Illustrative examples for about 18 g and a 1:2 ratio. 25–30 seconds is a common starting point, not a universal standard. Channeling and other factors also affect flow.

04 / Bayesian calibration

Learn the grinder on your counter.

The same number on two grinders does not mean the same thing. YanMore Grind uses a personalized Bayesian model, combining eligible measured brews with beans, equipment and recipe context to inform the next adjustment.

Guidance changes with valid records. Sparse or conflicting feedback may require more measured brews. There is no promised number of cups to calibrate, and no number can answer whether you like the taste.

Variety, roast, rest time, dose, distribution, tamping, temperature, pressure and equipment condition also affect extraction. Validate recommendations in the next brew; they do not guarantee flavor or replace taste.
