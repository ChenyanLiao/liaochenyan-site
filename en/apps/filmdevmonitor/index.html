A calm instrument for the darkroom

FilmDevMonitor

Once development begins, stop searching a note for the next step.

FilmDevMonitor keeps film, chemistry, temperature, agitation, and timing in one sequence.

A timer knows only the clock. This app also remembers which film, which chemistry, and which stage is running.

01 / Why it exists

The darkroom problem is never only the countdown.

Once chemistry enters the tank, time becomes very concrete. When to agitate, which solution comes next, whether the working volume is enough, and whether this process belongs to this film cannot wait until the timer rings.

A phone stopwatch says how many seconds remain, but not whether those seconds belong to developer, stop, or fixer. It also does not say whether the time came from a manufacturer sheet, a community recipe, or a personal note. Paper restores that context, but wet hands are poor page turners.

FilmDevMonitor therefore places preparation, sources, and timing in one order. It does not handle chemicals or guarantee an identical result for every roll. It simply keeps the busiest moment from becoming a jump among memory, timer, and scattered notes.

03 / Design philosophy

Let the screen say less without losing a step.

A darkroom is not the place for long paragraphs. Current stage, remaining time, and next action must be found at a glance; the rest opens when needed. The interface therefore does not make every function compete for the brightest position.

The dark display responds first to a working condition, not a decorative theme. Sources and safety notes are not hidden in distant help: when a time or dilution appears, its origin should be close enough to check.

The app does not replace manufacturer manuals, safety data sheets, local chemical rules, or experienced supervision. Gloves, ventilation, waste handling, and chemical compatibility remain the user's responsibility.
