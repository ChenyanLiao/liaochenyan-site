A calm instrument for the darkroom

FilmDevMonitor

Keep the process in view.

FilmDevMonitor brings preparation, timing, monitoring, and reference context back into one development sequence.

Designed for silver-gelatin work that depends on order and attention.

01 / Why it exists

Timing is only one part of development.

Film development asks the operator to hold process, chemistry, temperature, agitation, working volume, and the current stage in mind at once. A generic timer can count down, but it rarely explains why the wait matters, what comes next, or where the reference originated.

FilmDevMonitor began by returning the clock to its process context: conditions are visible during preparation, stages remain ordered while running, and the supporting references stay available for review.

The app is meant to protect concentration rather than interrupt the darkroom with more alarms. It also avoids presenting one reference as a guarantee for every film, chemistry, and equipment combination.

03 / Design philosophy

Keep attention on the film in your hands.

FilmDevMonitor arranges easily scattered steps and references into a quiet timeline. Clear stages, readiness states, and operating controls make the current task legible in a low-light workspace.

The dark display is a restrained response to the working environment rather than decoration. Source and safety context sit beside the procedure so reference material and active work do not drift apart.

The software does not replace manufacturer manuals, safety data sheets, local chemical rules, or experienced supervision.
