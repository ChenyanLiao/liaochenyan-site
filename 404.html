01Research Perspective

Questions before answers

A broad view of the scientific questions that shape my work, without publishing work in progress.

Science advances not only by producing answers, but by improving the questions we are able to ask.

Living systems are complex because causes and consequences cross scales. Computation can help organize that complexity, reveal patterns, and suggest hypotheses, but it cannot by itself decide what is biologically true.

I am interested in research that uses AI and quantitative models as instruments of inquiry: tools that make uncertainty visible, help prioritize evidence, and direct attention toward experiments that can genuinely change what we know.

01Preparing for App Store release

GrindDialing

A private calibration companion that turns brewing context and taste observations into a practical next grind adjustment.

Why I started it
Dialing in coffee is rarely about finding one universal number. Grinder scales, adjustment systems, brew methods, beans, baskets, recipes, and taste all change what the next useful step should be. GrindDialing began as a way to keep those observations together instead of scattering them across memory, screenshots, and handwritten notes.
How it is used
Choose the grinder and brew context, record the recipe and cup result, review a suggested direction and usable range, then save feedback for the next brew. History, sharing, and local export help turn isolated cups into a repeatable personal calibration practice.
Where it can go
The goal is a quiet, offline-first tool that supports a brewer's judgment rather than pretending taste can be reduced to a one-tap answer. It is being prepared for App Store release.
02Preparing for App Store release

FilmDevMonitor

An offline film-development console for coordinating stages, time, temperature, agitation, volume, and source-aware process references.

Why I started it
Film development asks the operator to watch several things at once, while generic timers and paper notes separate the clock from the process context. FilmDevMonitor began as an attempt to make a complete development sequence easier to follow, review, and repeat without turning the workspace into a collection of disconnected alarms.
How it is used
Select a process family, compatible film, tank, format, agitation mode, and working volume; then follow the ordered stages with start, pause, resume, and reset controls. Source confidence, compatibility checks, and safety notes keep reference information beside the active workflow.
Where it can go
The app is intended as a focused reference and workflow assistant for more consistent darkroom practice. It does not replace manufacturer manuals, safety data sheets, local chemical rules, or experienced supervision. It is being prepared for App Store release.

05About

Across science, software, and observation

I am drawn to scientific problems that sit between scales, disciplines, and ways of knowing.

01My interests include AI for Science, AI for Biology, molecular design, and scientific software. I see them as connected by a common question: how can computational tools help us reason about complexity without hiding uncertainty?

02I value models that clarify what should be tested next, not systems that make experimental judgment seem unnecessary. A prediction becomes scientifically useful when its assumptions, provenance, and limits remain visible.

03A background across ecological, evolutionary, and molecular questions has made me attentive to context. Independent software and photography extend the same habit: observe carefully, record what matters, and build tools that support human judgment.
