GrindDialing

A compact dialing and follow-through companion for people who need momentum without losing their notes, intent, or rhythm.

  • Outcome tracking for the next useful action.

FilmDevMonitor

A calm darkroom timer and logbook for film development, made for repeatable recipes and fewer anxious glances at the clock.

  • Step timers for developer, stop, fix, wash, and dry.
  • Temperature, dilution, and agitation notes.
  • Batch records that make experiments easier to compare.
